The filmmaker

Behind the camera.

I’m a documentary filmmaker, director and cinematographer based in India, working across stories of people, businesses, culture, technology and change.

For more than a decade, I’ve worked across India and Asia — often going where the story is, rather than where the production is.

My work has taken me into factories, villages, boardrooms, laboratories, mines, mountains and kitchens; meeting people whose stories rarely make it into the frame.

In the fieldDirector / Cinematographer
The way I work

I’m interested in what happens behind the obvious.

A business is more than its product. A place is more than its landscape. And a person is more than an interview.

I like spending time in the field — observing, asking questions, finding the details and waiting for the moments that make a story feel real.

My approach sits somewhere between documentary filmmaking and visual journalism: curious, patient and grounded in the people I’m filming.
